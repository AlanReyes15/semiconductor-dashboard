Incident response for a data change

The six phases from the Week 2 plan, applied to the insider edit in the manipulated version.

  1. PreparationMFA on all edit accounts, role-based access, audit logging, versioned and encrypted backups, and a recorded SHA-256 for every approved file.
  2. Detection and analysisThe hash check and the "change vs. approved" rule fail. Responders diff the file against v1.0 to see which rows and columns changed and whether chart settings or source text were altered too.
  3. ContainmentPull the dashboard to the last approved version, suspend the account in the log, revoke its sessions and API keys, and copy the altered file aside as evidence.
  4. EradicationRemove the altered file, close the access path (phishing, weak MFA, shared key), and patch what was exploited.
  5. RecoveryRestore v1.0 from backup, confirm its hash matches, rerun the validation checks, and republish after review.
  6. Lessons learnedWrite up what changed, when, from which account, and which control caught it. Tighten edit rights and require a second approver.

Evidence that answers what, when, who and which systems: the audit log (account, time, IP), version history, file hashes, the backup copy, and dashboard config history for scales, colors and removed sections.

Main dataset

Verified
Name
Global semiconductor water withdrawals (file dated 2019-02-13)
Publisher
Purdue University Research Repository (PURR), from Frost and Hua 2019
Data year
Fab capacity for Q4 2016, annualized
Units
Liters per year; liters per cm² of chip; scarcity factor is unitless (0.1 to 100)
Coverage
1,020 records at 817 sites in 37 countries
Boundary
Fab feedwater plus water used to generate the fab's electricity. Excludes chip packaging, use phase and end of life.
Method
Modeled from capacity, node and technology-specific water and power factors, not metered readings
Validation
Checks on the Data integrity tab, SHA-256 recorded on approval
Known issues
15 exact duplicate rows, 16 rows where parts do not sum to the total (under 0.5%), 4 rows with node 0 nm. Kept as published and flagged.

Assumptions and limits

  • Water figures are 2016 estimates. Capacity has grown since, so totals today are likely higher.
  • Emissions come from company and model studies, not from the facility file, so the two cannot be joined fab by fab.
  • Company totals use CDP reporting boundaries, which differ by company (Samsung includes appliances).
  • The market-based Scope 2 bars use the 17% average gap reported in the study, not each company's own figure.
  • The imec node results assume full abatement and an industry-average grid of 0.454 kg CO2e per kWh.
  • Packaging, transport and waste are outside every chart's boundary.
